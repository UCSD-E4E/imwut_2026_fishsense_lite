# Laser mount

The 3D-printed mount that holds the laser pointer on the camera housing, as
used for the deployments this paper reports. Printed in PLA (§3 of the paper).
3MF, units in millimetres; each file is a single part.

| file | part |
|---|---|
| `mount.3MF` | the mount body: a half-round cradle for the pointer (12.75 mm radius, so a pointer about 25.5 mm across), two screw bosses for the cap, and the foot that seats on the housing |
| `cap.3MF` | the clamp that closes over the pointer onto the cradle (same 12.75 mm radius), fixed by two screws |
| `taper.3MF` | a small tapered block with one bore |

Section 4.3 measures how far the laser moves between calibrations and names
three mechanisms seen in use, two of them in this part: the PLA flexing, and the
mount turning in the housing's cold shoe. The third, the pointer turning in its
clamp, is why a pointer carries an alignment mark against the mount.
